Adaptations
Selection of named anatomical surfaces; conversion to glTF/GLB and lossless
gzip fragments; recalculated normals; illustrative material colours;
original English and French educational summaries.
The clitoral, vestibular bulb, labial and female urethral supplements are original
mathematical educational reconstructions by Atom2Universe, also distributed
under CC BY 4.0. Published anatomical dimensions and spatial relationships guide
their illustrative shape; no third-party genital model was copied or traced.
They are individually labelled as reconstructions, not HRA donor segmentations.
Their parameter record and references are in female/provenance.json.
The source skin, distal vagina and bladder neck remain closed surfaces. The
native HRA bladder neck already overlaps the right pubic surface; the supplement
does not correct this source alignment. Labial bases overlap deeper teaching
surfaces instead of partitioning tissue volumes. Clitoral hood, vestibular
glands, pelvic-floor muscles and detailed vulvar nerves/vessels remain absent.

Only the contextual temporal bone is simplified for mobile rendering. The fine
ear structures are retained. Whole-body and local-ear datasets are separate,
not registered into one another. Full per-dataset element inventories, source
hashes, transformations, measured limitations and exclusions are recorded in
female/provenance.json and ear/provenance.json alongside their models.

The original authors do not endorse this application. These adaptations have
not undergone clinical validation and do not provide a complete anatomical atlas.
