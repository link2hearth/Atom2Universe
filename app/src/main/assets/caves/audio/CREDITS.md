# Cave World — sound credits

The shotgun (shotgun_0.wav to shotgun_2.wav) is the original project sound.
All other active effects are excerpts of the following published sound libraries.
Per-file source names, original/output SHA-256 hashes, cuts and processing are in sources.json.

## CC0 1.0

- Kenney — Impact Sounds: footsteps, hurt and boss cue. The kill ding uses built-in Sonivox.
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney — The Free Firearm Sound Library:
  1911 pistol, Carl Gustav M45, Marlin 336; shared player/soldier gun, SMG and rifle shots.
  https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
- artisticdude — Battle Sound Effects: Bow.wav and swish_4.wav (bow and sling).
  https://opengameart.org/content/battle-sound-effects
- mikewest; edited by AntumDeluge — Sheep Baa: animal_sheep_0.wav.
  https://opengameart.org/content/sheep-baa

## Changes

Selected excerpts, conversion to mono 16-bit PCM at 22,050 Hz with anti-alias filtering,
DC offset removal, gain balancing and short edge fades. No synthesized layers are added.
Footsteps additionally use a 1200 Hz low-pass filter without makeup gain.
The original source authors do not endorse this project.
Credits and license links are also accessible from the Cave World menu.
